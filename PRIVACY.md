# StageCharts Privacy Policy

StageCharts is a chord-chart app for worship teams (iPad and Mac). It is designed to keep your data on your own devices and in your own accounts.

## What StageCharts stores
- Your song charts, set lists, keys, capo settings, sticky notes, tempos and MIDI settings.
- These are stored on your device, and — only if you choose — in a folder you pick or in your own Google Drive.

## Google Drive
If you choose **Sign in with Google**, StageCharts uses Google's `drive.file` permission. This lets StageCharts see and change **only the files it creates** in your Google Drive (the "StageCharts Library" folder). It cannot see your other files.
- Your Google sign-in token is stored in your device's Keychain.
- StageCharts sends your library only to your own Google Drive. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else.
- You can sign out at any time in Settings, and revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Other connections
- **Band follow mode** shares the current set list and song changes directly between devices on your local network.
- **MIDI** messages are exchanged with devices and apps you connect (e.g. Ableton Live, MultiTracks Playback).
- **Software updates (Mac):** the app checks GitHub for new versions; this sends your app version to GitHub.

## What StageCharts does not do
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or accounts with the developer.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties.
